Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions — Ipca HR Recruitment App
1. Introduction and acceptance of terms
Ipca Laboratories Limited ("Ipca", "we", "us", "our", "Company") operates this HR Recruitment application ("App") to manage job applications from candidates. This document sets out, in a single continuous statement: (a) our Privacy Policy, explaining how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in compliance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"); and (b) our Terms and Conditions, governing your use of the App.
By registering on or using the App, you agree to be bound by both the Privacy Policy and the Terms and Conditions set out below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. This document applies to all candidates who register, apply, or interview for a position at Ipca through the App.
2. Eligibility
You must be at least 18 years of age and legally eligible to work in India to use this App. You must provide accurate, current, and complete information during registration and throughout the application process.
3. Data we collect
We collect the following categories of personal data when you use this app:
•	Identity data: name, date of birth, PAN number, passport number (if applicable), face photograph, and identity verification details obtained through Aadhaar-based DigiLocker / offline e-KYC in the form of a masked Aadhaar / Aadhaar Reference Number only
•	Contact data: mobile number, email address, residential address
•	Education data: academic qualifications, institutions attended, marks obtained
•	Employment data: previous employers, designations, salary history, experience letters, pay slips
•	Biometric data: face scan (via Aadhaar / DigiLocker) for identity verification only
•	Application documents: resume, marksheets, degree certificates
•	Health/Medical data: where required and permitted by law, information relevant to fitness for work, workplace safety, statutory requirements, or employee benefits
4. How we use your data
Your personal data is used exclusively for the following purposes. Each purpose below is linked to the specific data categories used for it, as required under the DPDP Act:
•	Identity verification, as required under applicable law — uses Identity data and Biometric data
•	Processing your job application at Ipca Laboratories — uses Identity, Contact, Education, Employment data and Application documents
•	Communicating with you about your application status, interview schedules, and offer letters — uses Contact data
•	Conducting background verification, by authorised vendors only — uses Identity, Education and Employment data
•	Meeting legal and regulatory obligations — may use any category above, as required by the applicable law
•	Protecting Ipca's premises, systems, and personnel — uses Identity data
•	Managing or defending legal claims and disputes — may use any category above, where relevant

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your current recruitment process. 
5. Consequences of not providing your data / withholding consent
Providing certain information is necessary for us to process and evaluate your job application. If you do not provide the personal data or consent requested, we may be unable to process your application, verify your identity, or consider you for the relevant role.
6. Data sharing
We share your data only on a need-to-know basis, for the purposes stated in Section 4, with:
•	Aadhaar / DigiLocker services for identity verification (as required by law)
•	Authorised background-check and recruitment agencies under strict confidentiality agreements
•	Ipca's group companies, subsidiaries, and affiliates
•	Authorised consultants and professional service providers
•	IT, HRMS, and other technology service providers
•	Auditors, legal advisors, and other authorised service providers
•	Government, statutory, or regulatory authorities, where required or permitted by law
We never sell your personal data to third parties.
7. Data retention
If your application is successful, your data will be retained as part of your employee record in accordance with our employee data policy.
If your application is unsuccessful or withdrawn, your data will be retained for a period of 12 months from the date of closure of your application, after which it will be permanently deleted from our systems.
You will be notified at least 48 hours before your data is scheduled for deletion, giving you the opportunity to exercise your data rights (see Section 11 below) before deletion takes place.
8. Minors
This App is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age and legally eligible to work in India, as set out in Section 2 (Eligibility) above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a minor without verifiable parental/guardian consent, we will take steps to delete such data.

9. Personal data you provide about others
If you provide us with personal data relating to any other individual — for example, a referee, an emergency contact, or a former employer's HR contact — you confirm that you are authorised to share that information with us for the stated recruitment purpose, and that you have informed that individual accordingly.
10. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023
As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:
•	Right to access: request a summary of your personal data we hold
•	Right to correction and completion: request correction of inaccurate data, or completion of incomplete personal data
•	Right to erasure: request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
•	Right to withdraw consent: withdraw your consent at any time; this will not affect any processing done before withdrawal
•	Right to grievance redressal: raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer / Data Protection Officer
•	Right to nominate: nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity
To exercise any of these rights, go to Account → Privacy & Security → Your Data & Rights within the app, or contact our Grievance Officer / Data Protection Officer directly (Section 23 below). We will respond to your request within __________[Insert: e.g. 15 business days] of receipt.
11. Data security
We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including:
•	Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS / TLS)
•	Secure server infrastructure with access controls
•	Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
•	Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act and its rules.

•	Providing false or another person's identity information may result in immediate disqualification from the recruitment process and, where already employed, appropriate disciplinary action under Ipca's applicable service rules, and, where applicable, legal action.
